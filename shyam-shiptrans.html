About The Company

A Legacy of
Logistical Excellence

Shyam Shiptrans Services India Private Limited is a distinguished logistics enterprise committed to delivering world-class warehousing and transportation solutions across the length and breadth of India.

We understand that behind every shipment is a business, a promise, and a livelihood. Our operations are built on the twin pillars of reliability and precision — ensuring your cargo reaches its destination intact, on time, every time.

From the snow-capped highlands of the north to the coastal ports of the south, from the industrial corridors of the west to the agricultural heartlands of the east — we navigate India's diverse geography so you don't have to.

What We Offer

Integrated Logistics
Solutions

Two core pillars of modern commerce — storage and movement — executed with meticulous precision.

01

Warehousing
Solutions

State-of-the-art storage facilities strategically located across India's major commercial and industrial hubs. Our warehouses are engineered for safety, efficiency, and accessibility — keeping your inventory secure and management seamless.

  • Multi-tier racking & bulk storage systems
  • Temperature-controlled & dry storage options
  • 24×7 security surveillance & access control
  • Real-time inventory management support
  • Flexible short-term and long-term leasing
  • Customs bonded warehouse capabilities
02

Transportation
Services

A robust, pan-India transportation network designed to move your goods with speed, safety, and precision. Whether it's full truckload, part-load, or express freight — our fleet and partners are always ready.

  • Full Truckload (FTL) & Part Truckload (PTL)
  • Express & time-definite freight services
  • Over-dimensional cargo & project logistics
  • Container and trailer transport solutions
  • Door-to-door delivery across all Indian states
  • Dedicated fleet for high-value shipments
03

Integrated
Logistics Support

Last-Mile Delivery

Supply Chain Mgmt
End-to-end supply chain visibility and management tailored to your business needs.
Cargo Packaging
Professional packing and labeling to protect your goods through the entire journey.
